# ScaleOps Media Website — Codex Operating Instructions

## Objective
Maintain a fast, SEO-ready, conversion-focused ScaleOps Media website without changing approved brand direction or fabricating business claims.

## Non-negotiables
- Preserve the dark futuristic ScaleOps visual system: deep navy/black, electric blue/cyan, restrained violet glow.
- Do not invent client names, revenue, ROAS, leads, testimonials, case-study outcomes, pricing, or certifications.
- Keep `assets/images/contact-hero-s.svg` as the featured graphic on the Contact page unless explicitly instructed otherwise.
- Keep all pages responsive and accessible.
- Do not expose secrets or credentials in the repository.
- Do not delete production content or alter DNS/account security without explicit human approval.

## Pages
- `/`
- `/systems/`
- `/services/`
- `/case-studies/`
- `/insights/`
- `/contact/`

## Before every deploy
1. Validate all internal links.
2. Confirm all required pages load.
3. Check page titles and meta descriptions.
4. Check images have alt text or appropriate decorative treatment.
5. Check mobile breakpoints.
6. Confirm no placeholder metrics were accidentally presented as real claims.
7. Update sitemap only after the production domain is known.

## Deployment
Use GitHub Pages/custom domain after the repository and domain are configured.
