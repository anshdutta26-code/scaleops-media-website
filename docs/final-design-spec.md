# ScaleOps Media — Final Immersive 3D Design Specification

## Visual system
- Brand source of truth: approved ScaleOps brandbook.
- Central visual anchor: a fixed cinematic orbit with holographic platform, particles and 3D depth.
- Opening state: exact ScaleOps icon-only mark inside the orb.
- Commercial-value state: mark morphs to a 3D ₹ symbol.
- Closing state: ₹ returns to the icon-only ScaleOps mark. No wordmark, tagline or brand name is attached to the final center object.
- Favicon: icon-only mark.

## Content architecture
1. Hero — customer problem + positioning.
2. Growth orbit — Acquire, Convert, Retain, Operate, Scale.
3. Logo → ₹ transition — attention becomes commercial value.
4. Systems hub — Acquisition, Conversion, Lifecycle, Automation, Analytics, Growth Operations.
5. Services — Paid Acquisition, Funnel/CRO, Authority Content, CRM/Lifecycle, Analytics, Growth Operations.
6. Proof — verified founder-led outcomes only.
7. Case studies — operating problems and systems built around the constraint.
8. Process — Diagnose, Design, Deploy, Optimize, Scale.
9. Authority content — operator-led insight topics.
10. Return transition — ₹ → ScaleOps mark.
11. Final CTA — logo-only center object plus strategy-call CTA.

## Section-specific orbit graphics
- Hero: towers, HUD, cubes, particles.
- Growth orbit: five orbital process planets.
- Value transition: beams + Offer / Follow-up / Revenue chips.
- Systems: six connected system nodes around the ₹.
- Services: 3D service stack + Paid / CRO / CRM / Content modules.
- Proof: growth chart, bars and proof chips.
- Case studies: connected system/network artwork + floating cubes.
- Insights: data-server artwork + authority-content chips.
- Return: converging arcs and particles.
- Final: halo + exact icon-only mark.

## Typography
- Desktop hero heading reduced from the previous oversized build.
- Section headings use a smaller premium hierarchy.
- Body text prioritizes readability and commercial clarity.
- Mobile/tablet sizes scale down independently instead of shrinking the desktop composition.

## Responsive behavior
### Desktop
- Three-zone composition: left authority copy, protected center orbit lane, right supporting content.
- Center orbit never receives overlaid body text.

### Tablet / iPad
- Orbit shifts into a sticky visual stage.
- Supporting content stacks without covering the stage.
- Reduced graphic scale and typography.

### Mobile
- Single-column reading flow.
- Orbit remains visible as a sticky cinematic stage.
- Secondary HUD artwork is reduced to prevent visual crowding.
- Navigation collapses to mobile menu.

## Conversion / lead backend
- Contact form subject must remain: `New ScaleOps Media Website Lead`.
- Available time windows: 12 PM, 1 PM, 2 PM, 3 PM, 4 PM, Flexible.
- Existing FormSubmit routing remains unchanged unless explicitly requested.

## Guardrails
- No numbered internal design labels in customer-facing copy.
- No unsupported/fabricated metrics.
- No generic stock-agency imagery.
- No separate brand name or tagline under the final central logo.
- The orbit remains the primary visual system throughout the experience.
